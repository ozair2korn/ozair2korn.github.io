Page Number Extractor

Copy a Facebook page (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C) and paste it below, or upload the page saved with “Save page as… → HTML”. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Paste something and press Extract.
How it works

Phone numbers: finds tel: links and phone-shaped text (7–15 digits, with spaces, dashes, dots, brackets or a leading +), removes duplicates, and adds your default country code to local numbers when set. Dates, times and long IDs are skipped.

Page stats: finds counts next to words like followers, likes, following, talking about, check-ins, reactions, comments, shares and views, and expands 1.2K / 3M / 4B into full numbers.

All numbers: every number in the text with the words around it.
